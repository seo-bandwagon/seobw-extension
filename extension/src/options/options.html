Google results

What SEO Bandwagon adds to Google’s results pages: a bar under each result, with its position, figures and Analyze, and one panel with exports, Collect pages and the location simulator. Changes show on an open results page at once.

Figures under each result

To show figures, the extension sends SEO Bandwagon the address of each result on a Google results page you open, up to 10 at a time, with its site. Never your search terms, and never a request to Google. SEO Bandwagon answers with each result’s link and traffic figures from DataForSEO, its domain age from RDAP, and your own on-page checklist where you captured that page. It needs a connected account (free).

Free accounts get figures for 20 searches a day, Pro for 250. Figures already looked up cost nothing, and the extension keeps them only until you close the browser. See the privacy policy.
