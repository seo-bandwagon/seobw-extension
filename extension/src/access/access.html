Analyze from Google results

Allow access to check other sites

Analyze checks the result's page under it, so you stay on Google: the extension reads the page's HTML from your browser, without your cookies, with its site's robots.txt and llms.txt. No tab opens. Chrome lets the extension read pages on other sites only with access to all sites. This is asked once: after that, every Analyze runs straight through. Nothing it reads is sent to SEO Bandwagon.
