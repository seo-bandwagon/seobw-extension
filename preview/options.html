Google results

What SEO Bandwagon adds to Google’s results pages: keyword data for the search above the results, a bar under each result, with its position, figures and Analyze, and one panel with exports, Collect pages and the location simulator. Changes show on an open results page at once.

Figures under each result

To show figures, the extension sends SEO Bandwagon the address of each result on a Google results page you open, up to 10 at a time, with its site, and, when you ask for their figures, of a page you pin to compare with them and of the pages an AI Overview cites. Never your search terms with them (keyword data, below, asks for its own yes), and never a request to Google. SEO Bandwagon answers with each result’s link and traffic figures from DataForSEO, its domain age from RDAP, and your own on-page checklist where you captured that page. It needs a connected account (free).

Free accounts get figures for 20 searches a day, Pro for 250. Figures already looked up cost nothing, and the extension keeps them only until you close the browser. See the privacy policy.

Not turned on: nothing is sent.

Keyword data for your searches

To show keyword data, the extension sends SEO Bandwagon the words of a Google search you open, with its related searches and People also ask questions (up to 50 at a time), and that Google’s country and language: when you click Show keyword data, or with each search if you choose that below; and a search you add to your rank tracker with “Add to my keywords”. Never a result’s address with them, and never a request to Google. SEO Bandwagon answers with each one’s monthly searches, difficulty, cost per click, competition and 12-month trend from DataForSEO, and your own rank tracker’s history for the search. It needs a connected account (free).

Each search with new keyword data counts against your plan’s daily allowance; keyword data already looked up costs nothing, and the extension keeps it only until you close the browser. See the privacy policy.

Not turned on: no search is sent.

One site a line. Their results are marked “Your site” on Google. Kept in this browser only.

AI Overview study
For invited testers of the AI Overview study

For people invited to SEO Bandwagon’s AI Overview study, and only when you click Send to study on a search the study opened for you: that results page as its CSV export, which is the search term, each result’s position, address, site, title, snippet, breadcrumb, date and rating, the page’s search features, related searches and People also ask questions, the AI Overview’s text and the pages it cites, Google’s country and language settings, the capture time and the extension version; and, when you click Google stopped me, that Google stopped you that day. Never any other search or page, never figures or keyword data, and never a request to Google. It goes to the study, not into your account’s captures, history or keyword data.

Each term opens in a private window with Google set to the US and English and personalization off (hl=en, gl=us, pws=0). Chrome must allow SEO Bandwagon in private windows: Allow in Incognito, on the extension’s Details page. Join or leave the study on seobandwagon.com; turn sends off here at any time.

Not turned on: nothing is sent to the study.
